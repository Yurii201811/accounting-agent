# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants must be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome
different levels of experience, give actionable technical feedback, protect
private information, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, publication of another person's private information, and
sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue and review discussions,
project events, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock threads,
reject contributions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately where appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

Use GitHub's report-abuse and content-reporting mechanisms for harassment or
abuse, or contact a listed maintainer through their GitHub profile. Security
vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is itself a violation.
