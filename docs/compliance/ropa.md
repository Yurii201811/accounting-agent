# Record of processing activities (ROPA) working register

Status: PRELIMINARY — NOT APPROVED

This register is an unpopulated production template. It does not authorize
real-client data and does not establish controller/processor status or legal
basis. The accountable privacy owner must maintain the operational record and
make it available as required.

IMY states that controllers and processors should maintain written, electronic,
up-to-date records of processing. The applicable controller and processor fields
must be completed from the actual contracts, architecture and data map.

Source: [IMY, Föra register över behandlingar](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/fora-register-over-behandling/).

## Change and review controls

New data classes, providers, purposes, countries, model routes, support access,
record classes or retention changes require the data map, DPIA need assessment,
DPA/subprocessor register, transparency material and this ROPA to be reviewed
before activation. The operational ROPA must link to dated evidence rather than
copying secrets or raw documents.
