# Data processing agreement control matrix

Status: PRELIMINARY — NOT APPROVED

This matrix is a contract-review aid, not a DPA and not legal advice. It does not
authorize real-client data or appoint any party as controller or processor.
Qualified counsel and the accountable customer/provider representatives must
approve the exact agreement and processing description.

IMY states that controller/processor processing should be governed by a binding
written agreement and identifies controls including documented instructions,
confidentiality, security, subprocessors, rights assistance, assistance with
Articles 32–36 obligations, and end-of-contract handling.

Source: [IMY, Personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/personuppgiftsansvariga-och-personuppgiftsbitraden/personuppgiftsbitradesavtal/).

## Safety conditions

- A product plan, template or click-through acceptance cannot replace signed
  contractual review where required.
- No provider is activated before matching DPA/subprocessor/security/transfer
  evidence is accepted for the data classes it will receive.
- Customer instructions cannot make structurally forbidden accounting actions
  available or override applicable security and archive controls.
- Ambiguous instructions stop and are escalated to authorized humans.
